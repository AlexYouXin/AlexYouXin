## Hi, I'm Xin You 👋

Ph.D. student at the Institute of Medical Robotics (IMR), Shanghai Jiao Tong University. Previously a visiting scholar at CAMP, Technical University of Munich, and an intern at OpenMMLab, Shanghai AI Lab.

My research focuses on **AI for medical imaging** (CT / MRI): image and video synthesis, universal medical image segmentation, vertebral shape modeling, and temporal motion modeling for pulmonary CT and cardiac MRI. Recently I have focused on VFM / VLM / MLLM for medical scenarios.
